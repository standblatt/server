# Standblatt

Standblatt runs shooting competitions at the range: registration, lane assignment, result entry, awards, live rankings and the paperwork afterwards. It follows the rules of the Swiss Shooting Sport Federation (SSV/FST) and is used by clubs for events like an air pistol championship.

With [My training](my-training/index.md) every shooter also gets a personal shooting diary on the phone: log a session in seconds, see the trend, the best series and where the group sits.

![The event dashboard](images/screenshots/dashboard.png){ .screenshot }

## What it does

- **Registration at the desk**: find a shooter by name, license number or a scanned code, pick a lane and the programmes, print the lane sheet and the control strip.
- **Results**: enter the series, the totals and awards follow the programme's rules; combined programmes take their series from another programme.
- **Live rankings**: ranked by the SSV rules, on the staff pages and on kiosk screens in the hall, updated the moment a result is saved.
- **Documents**: lane sheets, control strips, result sheets, wreath cards and rankings as PDF, printed at the range through a small print agent.
- **Reports**: statistics, club competition, revenue, awards, special prizes, all as PDF and CSV.
- **My training**: a personal training log with fast capture, hits on the target, progress and goals; competition results from Standblatt events are added with one tap.
- **Every club for itself**: each club keeps its own shooters, ranges, events and users. The interface is available in English, German, French and Italian.
