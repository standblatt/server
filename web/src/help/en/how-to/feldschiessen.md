# Set up a Feldschiessen over a period

You need this when the Feldschiessen is shot over weeks, e.g. from the opening of the range until 31 August plus the official weekend, and the results come from the desk, from scorers at the lane or on paper. It needs the permission *Configure events* (see [Roles](../user-guide/users.md#roles)).

## Create the event {#event}

1. Under **Events**, click **New event** and choose **Feldschiessen**. It switches on lane assignment, awards, the club competition, kiosk displays and helper links.
2. Enter the **Name** and as **Date** the main day, e.g. the Saturday of the official weekend. Its year sets the age categories.
3. Tick the ranges under **Ranges**, e.g. *Rifle 300 m* and *Pistol 25 m*.
4. Under **Shooting days**, add the days of the official weekend with their shooting times.
5. Switch on **Period (season)**, enter the **First day** and the **Last day**, and under **Shooting times** tick the weekdays the range is open, with their times. The single days of step 4 add to the period or replace its times on that date.
6. Click **Create**.

## Add the programmes {#programmes}

1. Open **Configuration** → **Programmes** and add the programmes, from the editor or with **Add from …** (library, programme file or last year's event). One programme per distance; weapons with their own award limits, e.g. *Stgw 90* and *Stgw 57*, are variants of the same name.
2. Under **Prices (CHF)**, tick every age category that may shoot. For a free Feldschiessen, enter 0.
3. Under **Awards**, click **Add award** for each award, with its **Kind** and the minimum total per age category.
4. Click **Create**. On an event with several ranges, then choose the **Range** of the programme under **Use**; it is saved at once.

## Choose how the results come in {#results}

The ways can be mixed within one event.

- **At the desk:** keep the module *Lane assignment*. Register each shooter on a lane from the [Dashboard](../user-guide/dashboard.md) and enter the series under **Results** when the targets come back (see [Results](../user-guide/results.md)). Switch on *Lane sheet* and *Control strip* under **Modules** if they should print at registration.
- **At the lane:** create a scorer link for each group of lanes under **Helper links** in the configuration (see [Helpers](../user-guide/helpers.md#create)); the scorers enter the series on a phone or tablet.
- **On paper:** switch off *Lane assignment* under **Modules** if nobody is registered on a lane. Type the sheets in on **Enter results** (see [Enter results](../user-guide/results-entry.md#quick)), or import the file of the scoring system (see [Import results from the scoring system](import-results.md)).

Switch off what the Feldschiessen does not use, e.g. *Kiosk displays* or *Club competition*, under **Configuration** → **Modules**.
