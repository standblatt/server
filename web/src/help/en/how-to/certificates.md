# Print certificates for the best ranks

You need this when the winners of a programme or a classification get a certificate with their name and rank, e.g. ranks 1 to 3 at the prize giving. Creating the form needs the permission *Print forms*, printing *Run events* (see [Roles](../user-guide/users.md#roles)).

## 1. Create the certificate form {#form}

1. Open **Print forms** in the club part of the sidebar and click **New print form**.
2. Enter a name (e.g. *Certificate*), choose **Prints** *A rank* and a paper size such as *A4 portrait (210 × 297 mm)*, then **Create**.
3. From **Fields**, add what the certificate shows: **Name** from *Shooter*, **Rank**, **Ranking** and **Class** from *Rank*, **Total** from *Result*, and **Own text** for fixed words such as *Certificate*.
4. For a logo, **Upload image** in the image library below the list of **Print forms** first; it then appears in the group **Images** of the fields (see [Images](../user-guide/print-forms.md#images)).
5. **Save**, then check the result with **PDF preview**.

On paper with a pre-printed design, add a scan of it under **Scan of the empty form** and calibrate the printer as for the [army's lane sheets](army-sheet.md#calibrate).

## 2. Choose the printer {#printer}

Under **Ranges → Set up the printers** of the event's first range, choose the printer for **Certificates** and **Save** (see [Connect a printer](connect-printer.md)). Without it, the certificates open as one PDF.

## 3. Print the certificates {#print}

1. Open **Rankings** of the event.
2. Click **Certificates** in the header of the programme or classification. The button appears once the club has a form that prints a rank.
3. Choose the **Print form**, for a classification with several classes the **Class**, and the ranks with **From rank** and **To rank** (1 to 3 unless changed).
4. Click **Print certificates**, or **PDF** to open them.

![Certificates from a ranking](../images/screenshots/certificates.png){ .screenshot }

One page prints per ranked shooter. A programme's certificate knows the shooter's results too, e.g. the series.
