# Take over last year's event

You need this when an event comes back, e.g. next year's championship or the Feldschiessen of the next season, and should start with last year's settings and programmes instead of an empty event. It needs the permission *Configure events* (see [Roles](../user-guide/users.md#roles)).

Copy last year's event when the event comes back as it was. Save a template when the club runs the same kind of event several times or in different forms, e.g. an annual programme for air pistol and one for air rifle.

## Copy last year's event {#copy}

1. Under **Events**, open last year's event (closed events are under *Closed*).
2. Open **Configuration** → **Copy & close** and click **Copy** next to *Copy for next year*.
3. Check the **Name** (the year and a leading number like *42.* are counted up) and the **Date** (the same date one year later). The other shooting days and the period move by as many days as the date, so choose the date on the right weekday, e.g. the Saturday of the official weekend.
4. Click **Create copy**. The copy opens in its configuration.

## Start from a template of the club {#template}

1. Open the event that is set up well and go to **Configuration** → **Copy & close**.
2. Click **Save as template**, check the **Name** (it is suggested without the year) and click **Save template**. The template is listed under *Templates* below the events.
3. Next time, click **New event** under **Events**, choose the template below the kinds of events, enter **Name** and **Date** and click **Create**.

A template can be changed like an event, but it takes no registrations. **Delete** on its **Copy & close** removes it.

## Check the new event {#check}

The copy takes over the ranges, organizer, language, settings, modules, programmes with prices, awards, combinations and special prizes, and the classifications. Registrations, results, kiosk links and helper links are not copied. Before the first registration, check:

1. **General**: the shooting days, the period and the shooting times.
2. **Programmes**: prices, award tiers and limits, and which programmes are active. Changes are easiest now, before shooters are registered.
3. **Modules**: what this year's event needs.
4. **Kiosk displays** and **Helper links**: create new links and send them out.
5. **Reservations**: switch the [online booking](../user-guide/online-booking.md) on again; the copy takes over its hours, but not the link and not the deadline. Reservations belong to the range and are not copied.

What exactly is copied is listed in [Events](../user-guide/events.md#copy).
