# Hand out awards and cash prizes

You need this when shooters collect their wreath cards, prizes in kind and cash prizes at the desk and you want to know who still has something to collect. It needs the module *Awards* and the permission *Run events*, or a helper link of the results clerk.

## Hand out to a shooter {#handout}

1. On the **Dashboard**, find the shooter in the *Completed* list and click the *awards to hand out* badge. The results open with the awards of every programme.
2. Hand out what is listed: wreath cards, prizes in kind and cash.
3. Click **Awards handed out**. The registration now carries the time of the handout and a green *awards handed out* badge.

![Dashboard](../images/screenshots/dashboard.png){ .screenshot }

A shooter not in sight on the dashboard is found with ++f4++ and the search, or by scanning the code on the lane sheet (see [Dashboard](../user-guide/dashboard.md)).

## Take a wrong click back {#undo}

Click the green *awards handed out* badge on the dashboard, or **Take back** in the results, and confirm. The awards count as open again. Only club members can take a handout back, not helper links.

## See what is still open {#open}

Under **Statistics**, download the report **Awards to hand out** as PDF or CSV. It lists every award not yet collected with shooter, club, programme, kind and value, the number of shooters with awards to collect and the cash still to pay out.

## Let a helper hand out {#helper}

For a separate award desk without a club account, create a helper link with the role **Results clerk** (see [Helpers](../user-guide/helpers.md#create)). It sees the completed list and marks awards as handed out like the dashboard.
