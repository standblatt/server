# Logging a session

Tap the **+** button, or a discipline under **Start training** on the Today page. The session opens full screen with your last discipline already selected.

![Series totals on the number pad](../images/screenshots/my-train-series.png){ .screenshot .phone }

At the top you see the running total, the number of shots and the average per shot. Below that is a chip for every series. Tap a chip to correct that series, tap **+** to add one, or use **Remove series** for the current one. The minus and plus next to "10 shots" change the number of shots of the current series, e.g. for a 5-shot series at 25 m.

## Three ways to enter a series

Choose the input for the current series at the top right. You can mix them in one session, e.g. totals for the first four series and every shot for the last two.

**Series**
: Type the series total. As soon as no further digit could make the number valid, Standblatt takes it and jumps to the next series. "9", "3" gives 93 and moves on; a 100 needs "1", "0", "0". With decimal scoring the point is added for you: "1", "0", "2", "7" gives 102.7. **Next** takes the number as it is.

**Shots**
: Tap the value of every shot. With decimal scoring type two digits, e.g. "9", "7" for 9.7, or three for a ten, e.g. "1", "0", "4" for 10.4. After the last shot of a series the next series starts.

**Target**
: Tap where every shot hit. Standblatt computes the value from the target face of the discipline, with decimals if the discipline is scored that way. The magnifier switches between the black and the whole target. This is the only way to get the [group analysis](progress.md#group).

![Tapping hits on the target](../images/screenshots/my-train-target.png){ .screenshot .phone }

On a computer you can type the digits, **Backspace** and **Enter** on the keyboard.

## Save

When the last series is complete, or when you tap **Finish**, the save sheet opens. Everything in it is optional:

- **Training or competition**: competitions appear as their own dots in the trend and are compared with your training.
- **Weapon**: which of your [weapons](account.md#weapons) you shot with. A new session starts with the weapon of your last session in the same discipline; tap it again to clear it, or add a new weapon right there.
- **How did it feel?**: five faces from bad to great.
- **Tags**: short words for what was special, e.g. *new grip*, *tired*, *new pellets*. Progress compares the sessions with a tag to your average.
- **When**, **where**, **notes**. *Where* starts with the place of your last training.

![After saving](../images/screenshots/my-result.png){ .screenshot .phone }

After saving Standblatt tells you right away:

- whether the session is a **new personal best**, or which place it takes among your sessions with the same number of shots;
- how many points per shot it is above or below the average of the **30 days before**.

## Unfinished sessions and offline

The session is kept on the device while you type. If you close the browser or the phone locks, it comes back the next time you start a session.

If there is no connection when you save, the session is kept on the device and uploaded as soon as you are online again. A note on the Today page shows how many sessions are waiting. A retried upload never creates a session twice.

Waiting sessions belong to the account that typed them. If your login expired in the meantime, log in again and they are uploaded; another person logging in on the same device does not get them. If the server refuses one, for example because its discipline was deleted, a note shows the reason and you can **Discard** it. Only new sessions can be saved offline; changing a session needs a connection.

Once you opened the app online, it also opens without a connection and shows your last data.

## Changing or deleting a session

Open the session in the **Log** and use the pencil to change it, or the bin to delete it.
