# My training

My training is a personal shooting diary. Every shooter can use it, whether or not their club runs events with Standblatt. It answers one question: **does my training work?**

- **Logging takes seconds.** After a session you tap the + button, type the series totals on the number pad and save. Standblatt moves on to the next series by itself.
- **As detailed as you like.** Log only the series totals, every shot value, or tap every hit on the target. You can switch per series.
- **Feedback right away.** After saving you see how the session ranks among your sessions of the same length and how it compares with your last 30 days.
- **Progress you can read.** Trend, personal best, goal, series curve, shot values, the size and centre of your group, what your tags tell you and how your weapons compare.
- **Competitions come for free.** Results from events run with Standblatt are found by your license number and birthday once the club closes the event, and added with one tap, rank included.
- **Works at the range.** Install it on the home screen. Without signal, sessions are kept on the phone and uploaded later.
- **Your data stays yours.** Export everything as CSV at any time and delete your account whenever you want.

## Pages

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | start a session, this week's activity, your recent trend and sessions |
| **Log** | every session, filtered by discipline and kind, competition results waiting to be added |
| **Progress** | the analysis of one discipline, see [Progress explained](progress.md) |
| **Settings** | profile (name, birthday and license number for the competition match), own disciplines, weapons, app, export; **Account** for email, password, language, second factor and deleting the account |

On a phone the pages are in the bar at the bottom, with the + button in the middle. On a larger screen they are in the sidebar.

## Access and price

My training is free for every user of the server.

Club staff accounts can use My training too: open **My training** in the user menu of the club sidebar.
