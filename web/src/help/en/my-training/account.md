# Account

Every Standblatt user has My training: club users open it from the start page or the user menu, shooters without a club sign up themselves. Email address, password, language, second factor and deleting the account live on the [Account](../user-guide/login-language.md#account) page, which **Account** in the sidebar or **Settings → Account** opens.

## Sign up

Open **Create an account** on the login page. You need your name, your email address and a password of at least 10 characters. You are logged in right away.

![Sign-up](../images/screenshots/my-signup.png){ .screenshot .phone }

Standblatt sends you a link to confirm your email address. Until you confirm it, a note on the Today page reminds you; **Send again** sends a new link. The link is valid for seven days.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot your password?** on the login page. You get a link that is valid for one hour. Setting a new password logs you out everywhere. Logged in, you change the password under [Account](../user-guide/login-language.md#account).

Accounts created by a club admin log in with their user name instead of an email; they can add an email address under **Settings → Email**.

## Profile and competition results {#competitions}

Under **Settings → Profile** you can enter your **birthday** and **license number**. With both, Standblatt finds your results from events run with Standblatt by any club, for example the club championship. They show up in the **Log** under *Your competition results* once the club has closed the event, that is, once the ranking is final. Each result carries your **rank** in the programme (for example *Rank 12 of 87*). Choose the discipline and tap **Add**, or **Add all**. Added results become competition sessions with their series and keep the rank.

## Own disciplines

Six disciplines are built in: air pistol and air rifle 10 m, pistol 25 m and 50 m, rifle 50 m and 300 m. For anything else, e.g. a field programme, add **Own discipline** in the settings with:

- the usual number of series and shots per series,
- whole or decimal (up to 10.9) scoring and the highest value of a shot,
- optionally a target face for tapping hits.

## Weapons {#weapons}

Shoot more than one gun, e.g. two air pistols? Add them under **Settings → Weapons** with a name of your choice; the calibre does not matter. Choose the weapon when you save a session and [Progress](progress.md#weapons-compared) compares them. Renaming a weapon keeps its sessions. Deleting it keeps the sessions too, they just have no weapon any more.

## API tokens

Want to import sessions from another app or analyse your log with your own script? Create a token in your account with the preset **Training app**; see [API tokens](../user-guide/login-language.md#api-tokens).

## Install the app

Standblatt is a web app that can be installed:

- **Android / Chrome**: Settings → **Install**, or *Add to home screen* in the browser menu.
- **iPhone**: Share → *Add to Home Screen*.

Installed, it starts full screen. A long press on the icon offers *Start training*.

## Your data

- **Export all sessions (CSV)** downloads every series of every session.
- **Delete account** removes the account with all sessions, goals and settings after you enter your password. Accounts that belong to a club must first be removed from the club by its admin.
