# Tutorial: your first training session

In this tutorial you log one air pistol training in My training: two series typed on the number pad and one series tapped on the target. Then you look at it in **Progress**. It takes about five minutes; a phone is ideal, but a computer works too.

## 1. Create your account

Open Standblatt and click **Create an account** below the login. Enter your first name, last name, email address and a password of at least 10 characters, then tap **Create account**.

You are logged in and see **Today**. It says *Your log is empty — let's change that.*

If your club already gave you an account, log in instead and choose **My training** on *Where to?*.

## 2. Start the session

Tap **Log my first training**.

The session opens full screen. At the top you see the discipline *Air pistol 10 m*, below it the chips of five series of 10 shots, and the input **Series** is chosen. Leave everything as it is.

## 3. Type two series

Tap *9*, *2* on the number pad. Standblatt takes 92 for series 1 and jumps to series 2 by itself. Tap *8*, *9*.

![Series totals on the number pad](../images/screenshots/my-train-series.png){ .screenshot .phone }

Chips 1 and 2 show 92 and 89, and series 3 is the current one. At the top you see the running total *181* with *20 shots · Ø 9.05*.

## 4. Tap a series on the target

Tap **Target** at the top right. A target face appears in place of the number pad.

Tap the target ten times, where your ten shots of this series hit. Every tap puts a hit on the target and its value in the row above it.

![Tapping hits on the target](../images/screenshots/my-train-target.png){ .screenshot .phone }

After the tenth tap, chip 3 shows the total of your hits and series 4 becomes the current one. You stop here: series 4 and 5 stay empty and are left out when you save.

## 5. Save with a tag

Tap **Finish** at the top right. The sheet *Save session* opens; **Training** is chosen.

Under **Tags**, type *new grip* in the field **New tag** and tap **+** next to it. The tag appears above the field, highlighted. Tap **Save session**.

![After saving](../images/screenshots/my-result.png){ .screenshot .phone }

You see the total of the session with *30 shots* and the average per shot, and below it *Your first session in this format — the next one has something to beat.* Tap **Done** to return to **Today**: the session is listed under *Recent sessions*.

## 6. See your progress

Tap **Progress** in the bar at the bottom (on a computer it is in the sidebar). *Air pistol 10 m* is chosen.

![Progress of a discipline](../images/screenshots/my-progress.png){ .screenshot .phone }

- **Last 30 days** shows the points per shot of your session.
- **Sessions** shows *1* and *30 shots*.
- **Personal best** shows *–* with *50 shots*: it counts only sessions over the full 50 shots of the discipline.
- **Trend** shows your session as one dot. Every further session adds a dot, and the line through them shows where you are heading.
- **Series curve** compares your three series, **Shot values** counts the values of your ten tapped hits, and **Your group** shows where those hits sit on the target.

## What you have done

You have logged a session in two ways of entering it, tagged it and seen it in your progress. Log the next training the same way; once two sessions carry the tag *new grip*, **Progress** shows under *What your tags tell you* whether the new grip pays off.
