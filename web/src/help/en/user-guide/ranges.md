# Ranges

**Ranges** (admins) lists the shooting ranges of the club.

![Ranges](../images/screenshots/ranges.png){ .screenshot }

- **Name** and **Number of lanes**.
- **Out of service**: lane numbers that cannot be used, e.g. `3, 7`. They show grey on the dashboard and cannot be assigned.
- **Lane groups** (optional): parts of the range with another distance, e.g. a pistol range with `1-10` as *25 m* and `11-15` as *50 m*. The range stays one range with one print agent and shared reservations; the dashboard and kiosk show the groups as headings, and a programme tied to a group only offers its lanes at registration (see [Programmes](programmes.md)). A group used by programmes cannot be removed.
- **Printing** opens the printers and print agents of the range (see [Printing](printing.md)).

A range with events cannot be deleted. Lane reservations belong to the range and show in all of its events (see [Reservations](reservations.md)).
