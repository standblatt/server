# Ranges

**Ranges** (permission *Ranges and printers*) lists the shooting ranges of the club.

![Ranges](../images/screenshots/ranges.png){ .screenshot }

- **Name** and **Number of lanes**.
- **Out of service**: lane numbers that cannot be used, e.g. `3, 7`. They show grey on the dashboard and cannot be assigned.
- **Distances**: a hall with 25 m and 50 m lanes is set up as two ranges, e.g. *Pistol 25 m* and *Pistol 50 m*, each with lanes from 1. An event can be shot on both (see [Events](events.md)), a programme can be tied to one of them (see [Programmes](programmes.md)), and one print agent can serve both (see [How printing works](../explanation/printing.md#range)).
- **Set up the printers** opens the printers and print agents of the range (see [Printing](printing.md)).

To connect a printer step by step: [Connect a printer at the range](../how-to/connect-printer.md).

A range with events cannot be deleted. Lane reservations belong to the range and show in all of its events (see [Reservations](reservations.md)).
