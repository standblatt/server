# Registrations

A registration puts a shooter on a lane with the programmes they shoot. Open it from the dashboard search (**Register for …**).

![Registration dialog](../images/screenshots/registration.png){ .screenshot }

## Lane

Pick a free lane in the grid, or leave it empty: the shooter then waits until you assign a lane by editing the registration.

- Occupied and out-of-service lanes cannot be picked.
- A reserved lane asks for confirmation before it is used.

## Programmes

The programmes of the event with their price for the shooter's age category. Programmes without a price for that category are greyed out (*not for this age category*).

- **Variants** (same programme name) appear as a choice of one.
- A **combinable** programme offers *take N series from …*: tick it and pick exactly N series of the other programme. The other programme is added automatically if it is not chosen yet.
- The **total** at the bottom adds up the prices.

## Shooter already registered

If the shooter is or was registered at this event before, a notice lists those registrations with their programmes and a link to open them. Programmes already booked are locked (*already registered*, or *no attempts left* for programmes with several [attempts](programmes.md#attempts)); a shooter may still shoot another variant in a separate registration.

## Printing

*Print lane sheet* and *Print control strip* are ticked by default (they only appear while the modules *Lane sheet* and *Control strip* are on, see [Modules](events.md#modules)): the documents print on the range's printers right after registering, or open as PDF if no printer is set up (see [Printing](printing.md)).

## Editing and deleting

Open a registration from the dashboard (**Edit**) or from the results (*Edit registration*) to change the lane or the programmes. Programmes that already have results cannot be removed; changing the picked series of a combination copies the new series. **Delete** removes the registration with its results after a confirmation.
