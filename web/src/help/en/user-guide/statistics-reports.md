# Statistics and reports

**Statistics** shows the key figures of the event and offers all reports.

![Statistics](../images/screenshots/statistics.png){ .screenshot }

- Registrations, completion rate, programmes sold, revenue, awards and their value.
- Participation and revenue per programme, registrations per age category, awards given.
- The **club competition** with ranked, out-of-competition and not ranked clubs (rules: [Club competition](../reference/rules.md#club-competition)).

## Reports

Every report is available as **PDF** and **CSV**; which reports there are and what they contain: [Reports](../reference/documents.md#reports). Awards still to collect: [See what is still open](../how-to/hand-out-awards.md#open).

Below the reports, the ranking of every programme can be downloaded as PDF or CSV.
