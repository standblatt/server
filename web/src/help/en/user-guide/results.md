# Results

Open the results of a registration with the **Results** button on the dashboard or by scanning the code on the lane sheet.

![Results dialog](../images/screenshots/results.png){ .screenshot }

## Entering series

- The cursor starts in the first empty series. As soon as another digit would exceed the series maximum, it moves on to the next field, so a sequence like `96 93 97 88` can be typed without the mouse.
- Series that track mouches have a second field for them.
- Series with single shots show a field per shot under the series; the series becomes their sum. Enter every shot or none: without shots, the series total is typed as usual.
- ++enter++ or **Save** stores the programme. Values above the maximum are marked and cannot be saved.
- Saving again replaces the results; the chosen award stays as long as the new total still reaches it.

A combined programme shows which series it takes from its source programme and fills in automatically once the source has results.

When every programme has results, the registration is completed and its lane is free again. **Print result sheet** prints an A5 summary for the shooter.

## Awards

Once a programme is complete, the awards the total reaches in the shooter's age category are listed, the most valuable first; of several wreath cards (or several prizes in kind) only the most valuable one reached is offered. Saving the results already gives the most valuable one; picking another one saves it at once. For a wreath card, **Print wreath card** prints the shooter's data onto a pre-printed A6 card; the value is printed only if the event setting *Print the award value on wreath cards* is on (in figures and in words).

## Handing out awards {#handout}

When the shooter collects the wreath cards, prizes and cash at the desk, press **Awards handed out** in the results dialog, which lists the awards of every programme; the *awards to hand out* badge in the *Completed* list of the dashboard opens that dialog. The registration then carries the time of the handout and a green *awards handed out* badge; until then it shows *awards to hand out*. A wrong click is taken back with **Take back** in the results dialog or by clicking the green badge on the dashboard (club members only, not helper links); the same works for the *ammo* badge. The report **Awards to hand out** (see [Statistics and reports](statistics-reports.md)) lists what is still open and the cash to pay out. A results clerk with a [helper link](helpers.md) may mark awards as handed out too.
