# Users

**Users** (admins only) lists the accounts with access to the club and their role.

![Users](../images/screenshots/users.png){ .screenshot }

- **Invite by email**: paste one or many addresses (comma separated or one per line, up to 300) and choose the role. People without an account get a mail with a link to set their password (valid for seven days) and then log in with their email address; existing accounts are added to the club and told by mail. The list under the form says what happened to each address. Mails leave at about four per second, so a large list takes a minute.
- **Add user**: the older way for an account without email: an existing account is added by its user name; for a new account also enter a password (at least 10 characters).
- **Role**: *Admin* manages ranges, events, programmes and users; *Staff* runs the events (see [Concepts](../getting-started/concepts.md#clubs-users-and-roles)).
- **Remove** takes away the access to this club; the account itself stays and keeps its other clubs.

Accounts are global: deleting an account is done by the platform operator in the [admin panel](../admin-guide/admin-panel.md). A forgotten password is reset with **Forgot your password?** on the login page if the account has a confirmed email address, otherwise by the operator.
